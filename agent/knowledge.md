# Sunrise Dental Clinic

## Contact and location
- Address: 214 Maple Avenue, Springfield. Free parking behind the building.
- Main phone: 555 010 2000
- Email: hello@sunrisedental.example

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 8 AM to 6 PM
- Saturday: 9 AM to 1 PM
- Sunday and public holidays: closed

## Team
- Dr. Maya Patel: general and family dentistry
- Dr. James Okafor: orthodontics (braces and clear aligners)

## Services
- Checkups and cleanings, fillings, crowns, root canals, extractions
- Teeth whitening
- Braces and clear aligners
- Same-day emergency visits during opening hours

## New patients
- New patients are welcome. Please arrive 15 minutes early to fill in forms, and bring a photo ID and your insurance card.

## Insurance and payment
- Most major PPO dental plans are accepted, including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, and MetLife.
- No insurance: a new-patient exam with cleaning and X-rays costs $149.
- Payment plans are available for treatments over $500.

## Policies
- Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.
- Children under 16 need a parent or guardian at their appointment.
